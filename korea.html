Next Stop: Korea

After I graduate in May 2027, I'm moving to Busan, South Korea, to chase my biggest 꿈 (dream): teaching English. I'm earning my 120-hour TEFL certificate and applying to EPIK, the English Program in Korea, which places teachers in public schools across the country.

Becoming a 선생님 (teacher) in Korea means everything I've worked toward for the past ten years is finally coming together. It all started with nervous greetings at Korean restaurants, and now I'll get to help students learn a new language, just like I did.

If you're dreaming about something big, start small and stay persistent. It really does start with you!
